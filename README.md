Live Equity Monitor
Fundamentals, earnings, valuation and an AI analyst for any listed company, pulled up from its ticker.

Company view

Key drivers: an automatic summary of where the company is growing, where margins are moving and what’s driving results right now.
Financials: income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, quarter on quarter or year on year.
Segment growth: revenue by business unit and region, with % change and each one’s contribution to total growth.
Margins and costs: gross, operating and net margin trends, plus the cost structure as a share of revenue.

Earnings

Next-quarter forecast: consensus revenue and EPS alongside an in-house model estimate.
Expectations vs actuals: beat/miss history with surprise % and implied growth.
Price reaction: OHLC candlestick charts ±30 days around any earnings date, showing the run-up, the reaction and the drift afterwards.

Peers and valuation

Peer comparison: growth, margins, P/E, EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales and FCF yield against a peer median.
Peer group: add or remove tickers as you like.
Charts: growth against valuation, to show who’s cheap or expensive relative to fundamentals.

Ask (AI analyst)

Questions in plain English: ask anything about the company or its peers, e.g. “what’s driving growth?”, “is it cheap vs peers?” or “what should I watch into the print?”
Grounded answers: Claude works from everything the monitor has loaded and fetches more on demand, such as other tickers, longer financial history or price data around earnings.
Desk-ready output: concise answers with tables and charts drawn inline, and follow-ups keep the context.
